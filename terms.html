1. What this site is

Thinking Like an Economist is an educational website maintained by Rupa Korde, hosting classroom games, teaching materials, and related content for teaching economics. It is not a commercial product and does not process payments.

2. Use of the games and materials

  • The games and materials on this site are made available for educational use, including in classrooms and self-study.
  • You may use and adapt them for teaching purposes. If you share an adapted version publicly, please credit the original source and this site.
  • Materials may not be resold or repackaged as a commercial product without permission.

3. Live classroom games

Some games (such as The International Trade Game) run as live web applications used during a classroom session, hosted on third-party infrastructure (such as Google Apps Script). During a session:

  • Students typically enter a display name or country name and a roll number, chosen by their instructor's session setup — no account creation or personal contact information is required to participate.
  • Session data (names, group choices, in-game transactions) is used only to run that session and for the instructor's own record-keeping, and is not shared with third parties beyond the hosting platform required to run the game.
  • Instructors running a session are responsible for complying with their own institution's policies on student data.
