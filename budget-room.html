The Budget Room

You are Gondor's Finance Minister. Sixteen programmes, one purse. Push spending up on what matters to you — but every extra euro above your fiscal headroom has to come from cutting something else, or from borrowing it.

GDP €3,200bn Currency: EUR
Briefing — Year 1

Growth has cooled to 1.4%. The opposition is split between demanding a real welfare expansion and demanding sharp cuts, and Gondor's bond markets have made clear they'll tolerate a deficit near current levels but will punish a sharp blowout. You have one budget to set the tone for the year — and four open questions about how you'll be judged for it, at right.
